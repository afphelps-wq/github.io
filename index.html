About me 

I'm a sophomore at Carnegie Mellon University studying computational biology and a systems immunology intern in Dr. Tullia Bruno's lab at UPMC Hillman Cancer Center. I am passionate about exploring the intersection between machine learning and the adaptive immune system. 

Most of my current work surrounds creating single-cell analysis pipelines to make that analysis reproducible and downstream biological conclusions are defensible.
